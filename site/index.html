The Deep Archive

How this works

Every vault is a file sealed with five locks, each lock a layer of encryption. A lock opens with a key made from the answers of the seals printed under it: little puzzles of every kind (ciphers, pictures, sound, files, code, the browser itself). Some locks are threshold locks: they are made from many seals, and any k of them open the lock, so a team can work on them in parallel. When you have decrypted a vault you find a line ANSWER:; type it on the page to solve the vault.

When every vault of a room is solved, the door of the room opens: its locks are made of the seals you earned. Solving a door opens the next room.

Nothing here can be skipped: later rooms are encrypted with what you earn in earlier ones. There are no scores and no clocks. Tools, code and teams are all allowed.

Working in a team: “Save progress” gives a code; “Load progress” on someone else's computer merges it (seals, notes and verified locks). Keep your own notes of answers; later vaults refer to earlier ones.

Progress and notes live in this browser only. Use “Save progress” now and then.
